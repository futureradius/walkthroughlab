# About this walkthrough

Walkthroughlab is a study prototype for experiencing architectural spaces from the inside, in a browser.

## Moving

- The joystick walks forward and back and turns on the spot.
- Dragging anywhere else looks around. While dragging, pushing the joystick sideways sidesteps.
- On a keyboard, W/S or the up/down arrows walk, A/D sidestep, and the left/right arrows turn.
