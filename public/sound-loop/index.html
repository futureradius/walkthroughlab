Sound loop test

Each sound below repeats forever. Each time it gets back to its start (the bar fills, then turns orange for a moment), listen for a click, a tick, a short gap, or a dip in volume. The chord comes back to its start every 8 seconds, the wind every 10. The first time comes 3 seconds after you press Play.

  • Use earphones if you can, at a comfortable volume.
  • “With overlap” is how the walkthrough will loop its sounds. “Plain” is there to compare with.
  • On an iPhone with the ringer switch set to silent you will hear nothing. That is expected: flip the switch to hear the sound.
  • Also try switching to another app and back. The sound should fade out, then fade back in where it left off.
