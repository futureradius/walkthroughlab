# Lamp colours follow Rhino's Raytraced view

The author tunes lamps by eye in Rhino's Raytraced view, and the bake has to throw the same light (#12, #29). Rhino stores a picked glow colour as typed, each channel ÷ 255, and its glTF exporter writes it unchanged. Raytraced, however, raises that colour to the power 2.2 before it lights anything, under the linear workflow every Rhino document starts with (research note `docs/research/light-from-glowing-surfaces.md`, section 10). So `npm run optimize` raises every lamp's colour to the power 2.2 too, and the bake and the walkthrough's glow use the raised colour. The colour Rhino shows is what the walkthrough shows. Taking the exported colour as linear was rejected: every lamp would bake brighter and paler than Raytraced shows it, and the author would have to tune lamps against something other than what they see. The gamma is fixed at 2.2 rather than read from the model, because the exporter does not write the document's linear workflow setting; the README asks authors to leave it at 2.2.

## Consequences

- The lumen optimize lists for each lamp, and the hex table for 2700 to 4000 K in the README, are computed from the raised colour, so they agree with what Raytraced shows.
- The match holds only under the README's conditions: no light objects or separate sun in Rhino, the same sky at intensity 1, no post effects, tone mapping's black and white points at 0 and 1.
- Compared on 2026-10-25 from the same spots, Raytraced against the walkthrough, for a 60 × 60 panel (4000 K), a 1.2 m tube (3500 K), a pendant (3000 K), a downlight (2700 K) and an elevator cabin's ceiling (4000 K): brightness and colour temperature matched closely enough for the author, on the laptop and on the Redmi. The differences that remain are decided elsewhere in #29, not colour: lamps glow from their front only where Raytraced glows from both sides, a bright glow shows its colour where Raytraced burns it to white (no bloom), and the bake is grainier around small lamps at its usual samples.
- Should a later Rhino change its gamma or its exporter start writing linear colours, `RHINO_GAMMA` in `src/lamps.ts` is the one place to change.
