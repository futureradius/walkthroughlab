Lamp calculator

What to set in Rhino for a lamp: the colour to paste for a temperature, and the intensity for the lumen of the real fixture. The sums are the ones npm run optimize lists lamps with.

Colour

Glowing area

The side that glows only, its front (Dir shows it); a closed solid's whole outside. Rhino's Area command measures it.

Brightness

Intensity is the emission material's intensity, or the multiplier of a Physically Based one's emission. Change either number and the other follows.

How it is worked out

lumen = intensity × luminance × 683 × π × area in m²

Luminance is how bright the colour is once Rhino's Raytraced view raises it to the power 2.2, from 0 to 1. Colours are those of a glowing hot body (Mitchell Charity's table, CIE 1931 2°). Rhino's own kelvin swatches are not. The match with Raytraced holds under the conditions in the README's Lamps section; check against the list optimize prints, and correct with new = old × wanted ÷ listed.
